ABOUT US

Crafting Furniture That Feels Like Home

At Furni Aura Living, we create premium custom furniture that blends craftsmanship, comfort and timeless elegance for every home.

OUR STORY

Luxury is for Everyone

Furni Aura Living was created with a simple belief that premium furniture should not be limited to a few. Everyone deserves beautifully crafted furniture that brings warmth, comfort and elegance into their home.

Based in Udaipur, we design and manufacture custom furniture using quality materials, skilled craftsmanship and modern techniques.

Every product is built with attention to detail so that it becomes more than furniture—it becomes part of your family's memories.

Our Mission

To make premium quality furniture accessible to every family by delivering handcrafted designs, exceptional quality and honest pricing.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted custom furniture brands, known for craftsmanship, innovation and customer satisfaction.
